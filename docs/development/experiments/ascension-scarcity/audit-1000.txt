# Ascension core-loop audit — 1000 seeds, 19 bots, rule sets v8 vs v9, ablations full, no-terrain-bonus, no-passives, no-reserves, no-strain, no-civ-in-crises, cap 60 rounds (815s)

  v8: rules v8: ready crises (+4 per round waited, strike after 2), no era budget, Invasion 40 + 2/open, no forest cover
  v9: rules v9 (as shipped): 70/70/90 cards per era (a card played costs 1, a discard 2, unspent carry over); Invasion 43 + 1/open, forests +2 each

## Bots
  poker-max    [score] highest poker score (chips × mult); ignores the world; faces crises at once
  score-max    [score] highest total immediate score; faces crises at once
  balanced     [balanced] 5 cards, always raising the lowest stat; faces crises at once
  terrain      [focused] focuses the stat its land favours most, plus what the era checklist asks for; faces at once
  civ-synergy  [score] maximises civilization passives once it has civilizations (balanced before); faces at once
  focus-V      [focused] naive: Vitality plus what the checklist asks for; faces at once
  focus-P      [focused] naive: Prosperity plus what the checklist asks for; faces at once
  focus-I      [focused] naive: Industry plus what the checklist asks for; faces at once
  focus-K      [focused] naive: Knowledge plus what the checklist asks for; faces at once
  random       [weak] seeded random legal play/discard; faces at once
  patient      [balanced] balanced play, but faces a ready crisis only once it would survive (or when it strikes)
  prepared     [balanced] balanced + reads the forecast: fixes what it lacks, holds back the checkpoint, faces when it would survive
  resolute     [balanced] reads the forecast; while short, plays only the suit that most improves it (discarding the rest); otherwise like prepared
  planner      [score] reads the forecast (reserves included): prepares with the best-margin play, otherwise the best score; faces when it would survive
  thrifty      [score] the planner, judging each play per unit of era budget it costs (hand size can matter)
  lean-V       [focused] reads the forecast; when safe plays only Vitality (then what the checklist needs)
  lean-P       [focused] reads the forecast; when safe plays only Prosperity (then what the checklist needs)
  lean-I       [focused] reads the forecast; when safe plays only Industry (then what the checklist needs)
  lean-K       [focused] reads the forecast; when safe plays only Knowledge (then what the checklist needs)
