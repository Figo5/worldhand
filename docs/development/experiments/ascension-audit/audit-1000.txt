# Ascension first-playable audit — 1000 seeds, 11 bots, 6 rule sets, cap 60 rounds (218s)

## Bots
  poker-max    highest poker score (chips × mult); ignores the world
  score-max    highest total immediate score (poker + land + civilization passives)
  balanced     5 cards, always raising the currently lowest stat
  terrain      focuses the stat its land favours most, plus what the era checklist asks for
  prepared     balanced, but reads the crisis forecast: builds what it lacks and holds back the checkpoint until safe
  civ-synergy  maximises civilization passive bonuses once it has civilizations; balanced before
  focus-V      focuses Vitality (♥) plus what the era checklist asks for
  focus-P      focuses Prosperity (♦) plus what the era checklist asks for
  focus-I      focuses Industry (♣) plus what the era checklist asks for
  focus-K      focuses Knowledge (♠) plus what the era checklist asks for
  random       seeded random legal play/discard (weak baseline)
